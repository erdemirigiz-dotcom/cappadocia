    FAQ

    Before you fly

    What if the weather turns?

    Flights are confirmed the evening before. If the wind says no, you rebook or receive a full refund.

    Is it safe?

    Pilots hold commercial licences and fly these valleys daily. Baskets are inspected before every flight.

    What should I wear?

    Layers and closed shoes. Mornings are cold; the flame above you is warm.

    How do I get there?

    We collect you from any hotel in Göreme, Uçhisar or Ürgüp — included in every package.

    Can I cancel my booking?

    Yes. Cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before your flight for a full refund; within 48 hours we rebook you to another morning.
